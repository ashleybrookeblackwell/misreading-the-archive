[underlined: MEMORANDUM] TO Mr. Heinrich, Chief MFA&A Section

SUBJECT: Legal ownership of archives and libraries

1. In stating legal ownership of archives and libraries there is a
difference between the big and the small institutions.

2. All [underlined: big] institutions have got the questionnaire for German archival
institutions and libraries and filled out the questions of "aquisitions sub-
sequent to 1 Jan 1938," of confiscated materials. The most institutions, the
Statearchives at Darmstadt and Marburg, the National archives and the City ar-
chives at Frankfurt, the Landlibraries at Darmstadt and Kassel, the University-
libraries at Darmstadt, Frankfurt and Giessen have reported no confiscated ma-
terials. The Statearchives at Wiesbaden had got documents exchanged with Nether-
landsarchives. They have already been returned to the Hague. The Library at
Fulda had reported several Jewish books, they have been fetched off. The books
of Jews, Freemasons, Catholic orders, French Castles deposited in the Landlibra-
ry at Wiesbaden have been reported long ago and returned in part. The University-
library at Marburg has stated a few books of French property. The investigation
has been begun and finished by Mr. Boell.

3. The result in Archives was [handwritten insertion above: presumed] to be little; for records have their fixed
places, the distribution is ruled by the principles of the records' provenance.
In [underlined: small] archives and libraries (of towns, villages, churches, families) it was
quite unlikely that confiscated materials could be found. Nevertheless Mr. Boell
made a test and sent out the forms of Statement of legal ownership to all commu-
nities and churches in the Regierungsbezirk Darmstadt. The result has been negative.
Mayor and parson have stated no confiscated materials. That the action has been
stopped. It would be a useless expenditure to continue it and to send questionnaires
to one or two thousand communities and churches without getting indications of
confiscated materials in this way.

[signature]

DR. SANTE
